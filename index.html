HPCL Pampore Plant • Safety Automation

Automated Bulk LPG Decanting & ESDV Interlocks

Proposed control scheme during internship at HPCL Pampore Bottling Plant to eliminate human error and manual venting risks during tanker unloading.

  • Electro-Pneumatic ESDVs: Central PLC actuation on liquid/vapor lines.
  • Differential Pressure Sensors: Prevents cavitation and liquid backflow.
  • Hydrocarbon Gas Detectors: Automated nitrogen purging at >10% LEL.
  • Earth Bonding Interlock: Prevents decanting until static earthing is confirmed.
