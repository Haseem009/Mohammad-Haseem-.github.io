HPCL Pampore Plant • Safety & Automation Architecture

Automated Decantation & Emergency Interlock System Scheme

Proposed a complete safety and operational control automation scheme for bulk LPG tank truck decantation during my internship at the HPCL Pampore LPG Bottling Plant to minimize human error and eliminate hazardous venting risks.

System Architecture & Interlock Sequence:

  • Electro-Pneumatic Emergency Shut-Off Valves (ESDV): Automated ROV (Remote Operated Valve) integration on liquid and vapor return lines controlled via a central PLC.
  • Differential Pressure & Level Transmitters: Real-time monitoring of tank truck pressure vs. static storage vessel pressure to prevent liquid backflow or cavitation during pump operations.
  • Gas Detector Interlock Array: Optical Infrared hydrocarbon gas sensors around decanting gantries configured to trigger automated ESDV closure and nitrogen purging upon detecting >10% LEL.
  • Automated Earth Bonding Interlock: Static grounding interlock relay prevents pneumatic valve opening until full electrical earthing continuity is verified.
